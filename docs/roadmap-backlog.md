- [#21](https://github.com/phyzess/pappra/issues/21) supports both adoption and collaboration; it is not merely a child of a comparable-tool checklist.

- [#14](https://github.com/phyzess/pappra/issues/14) supports knowledge adoption; it does not substitute for the later knowledge-reuse loop.

- [#20](https://github.com/phyzess/pappra/issues/20)'s observation and input-action parts should not force one another's implementation.
- [#17](https://github.com/phyzess/pappra/issues/17) is no longer an outstanding decision or prerequisite implementation. [#7](https://github.com/phyzess/pappra/issues/7) still needs its own work-run no-effect policy: refusing a message before any transport does not make the refused message eligible for automatic retry. Preserve [#17](https://github.com/phyzess/pappra/issues/17) regressions in both [#7](https://github.com/phyzess/pappra/issues/7) and [#20](https://github.com/phyzess/pappra/issues/20) changes; a test envelope must be legitimately accepted before it can exercise later transport behaviour.
- [#24](https://github.com/phyzess/pappra/issues/24) and [#26](https://github.com/phyzess/pappra/issues/26) may inform value measurement but are not prerequisites for continuing manual observation in [#34](https://github.com/phyzess/pappra/issues/34).

## What removal from the critical path means

Remove inappropriate critical-path requirements, duplicate future implementations and unsupported automatic conclusions. Do not delete history, drop known safety concerns or close unresolved defects as a consequence of reprioritization.
