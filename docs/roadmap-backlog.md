Preserve the #17 regressions when changing dispatch or delivery observation under [#20](https://github.com/phyzess/pappra/issues/20). A test envelope must be legitimately accepted before it can exercise transport behaviour.

## What removal from the critical path means

Remove inappropriate critical-path requirements, duplicate future implementations and unsupported automatic conclusions. Do not delete history, drop known safety concerns or close unresolved defects as a consequence of reprioritization.
