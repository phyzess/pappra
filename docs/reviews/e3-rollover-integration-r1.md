Specify exact durable request identity and immutable intent-input comparison, same-id/different-input conflict behavior, completed/aborted replay behavior, and how restart avoids a second successor launch between native creation and identity publication. `findHandoff` alone does not solve launch exactly-once behavior; unsupported crash windows must remain unknown/reconcile-required rather than retrying a launch.
